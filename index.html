About

I’m a passionate journalist with over 5 years of experience covering arts & culture, specializing in music and theater reviews and reported features. I enjoy using my reporting to illuminate the connections between art and the communities that create it. I am also an experienced digital content creator for nonprofit and academic organizations.

I recently earned my master's in journalism from Northwestern's Medill school as part of the magazine specialization. Coursework included Arts Criticism and Journalism, Audio Journalism: The Art of the Interview, Magazine Feature Writing, Magazine Editing, Foundations of Video Journalism and Foundations of Interactive Journalism. I hold an undergraduate degree from the University of California, Berkeley in media studies and political science.
